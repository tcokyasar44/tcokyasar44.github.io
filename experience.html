About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Engineering Technology and Industrial Distribution at Texas A&M University, where I also direct the Advanced Transportation Logistics & AI for Resilient Systems (ATLAS) Lab, which I co-founded with Dr. Sudipta Chowdhury, and a Research Affiliate in the Interdisciplinary Analysis Program of the Texas A&M Transportation Institute. Previously, I was a Computational Transportation Engineer at Argonne National Laboratory.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Nevsehir University in 2012. After two years at Kuwait-Turkish Participation Bank, I received a scholarship from the Republic of Türkiye Ministry of National Education to pursue graduate studies in the United States. I attended the Intensive English Program at the University of Georgia in 2014 and earned my M.S. (2016) and Ph.D. (2020) in Industrial Engineering from the University of Tennessee. During my Ph.D., I interned at Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Argonne National Laboratory. I also served as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering at Tarsus University.

I am a father of two: Alparslan and Gunes Azra.
