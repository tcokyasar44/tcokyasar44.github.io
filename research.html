Research

Optimization at the scale of real systems

My research develops large-scale optimization models and algorithms that make transportation and logistics systems more efficient, affordable, and resilient. I combine mathematical programming, decomposition, and high-performance computing with agent-based simulation to answer planning questions at metropolitan and national scale.

EV charging & electrification

Electrifying freight and passenger fleets is as much a planning problem as an engineering one. I develop location, capacity, and scheduling models that decide where chargers go, how many plugs each site needs, and how vehicles should detour and queue to use them, scaled to millions of trips with decomposition and parallel computing.

Representative work

  • Electric vehicle supply equipment location and capacity allocation problem. European Journal of Operational Research, 2024. Link
  • Extreme-scale EV charging infrastructure planning for last-mile delivery using high-performance parallel computing. Transportation Research Part B, 2026. in press Link
  • A national-scale EV charging scheduling framework: Optimal detour routing under infrastructure capacity constraints. preprint Link
  • Intercity EV charging readiness in Türkiye: A countrywide scheduling and economic assessment. preprint Link
  • A label-correcting algorithm for constrained one-to-many k-shortest path problem with replenishment. ANT, 2024. Link

Transit & electric bus fleets

Transit agencies are replacing diesel buses with battery-electric ones that have shorter ranges and need depot or on-route charging. My models jointly schedule vehicles and charging, place chargers under uncertain demand, and quantify how many buses a transition really requires, using public GTFS data from agencies such as those in Austin, Chicago, and Los Angeles.

Representative work

  • Heuristic solutions to the single depot electric vehicle scheduling problem with next day operability constraints. Transportation Research Part C, 2024. Link
  • Problem of locating and allocating charging equipment for battery electric buses under stochastic charging demand. European Journal of Operational Research, 2025. Link
  • Integrated optimization of scheduling and flexible charging in mixed electric-diesel urban transit bus systems. under review Link
  • Assessing the impact of route interlining via bus schedule optimization. EUSPN, 2025. Link
  • Impact of transit on mobility, equity, and economy in the Chicago metropolitan region. Public Transport. accepted Link

Freight & supply chains

Freight models need realistic decisions about who buys from whom and how loads are matched to carriers. I build and calibrate supplier-selection and market models inside agent-based freight simulation, and design routing models for time-constrained urban delivery.

Representative work

  • Modeling and calibration of the supplier selection problem in freight agent-based simulations. Transportation Research Record, 2026. Link
  • Time-constrained capacitated vehicle routing problem in urban e-commerce delivery. Transportation Research Record, 2022. Link
  • Additive manufacturing capacity allocation problem over a network. IISE Transactions, 2022. Link
  • Approximate dynamic programming for matching, scheduling, and pricing in truckload freight markets. submitted

Drones & last-mile delivery

Drones can cut delivery times but are limited by battery range. I design drone networks with automated battery-swapping stations, compare the regional energy use of drone and truck deliveries, and develop exact algorithms for routing mixed drone–ground fleets.

Representative work

  • Designing a drone delivery network with automated battery swapping machines. Computers & Operations Research, 2021. Link
  • Optimization of battery swapping infrastructure for e-commerce drone delivery. Computer Communications, 2021. Link
  • Comparing regional energy consumption for direct drone and truck deliveries. Transportation Research Record, 2023. Link
  • A branch-and-cut algorithm for routing a heterogeneous drone-ground vehicle fleet to deliver time-sensitive products. Transportation Research Part C, 2026. Link
  • Joint routing of conventional and range-extended electric vehicles in a large metropolitan network. Transportation Research Part C, 2022. Link

Shared, autonomous & multimodal mobility

Shared and autonomous vehicles change how people travel and how transit should be designed. Working with the POLARIS agent-based simulator, I study ride-matching at scale, household and shared autonomous vehicles, pooled rides, and redesign of transit networks around new mobility services.

Representative work

  • Optimal assignment for the single-household shared autonomous vehicle problem. Transportation Research Part B, 2020. Link
  • Dynamic ride-matching for large-scale transportation systems. Transportation Research Record, 2022. Link
  • Redesigning large-scale multimodal transit networks with shared autonomous mobility services. Transportation Research Part C, 2024. Link
  • Joint optimization of multimodal transit frequency and shared autonomous vehicle fleet size. Transportation Research Part C, 2026. Link
  • Proactive assignment strategy with human choice models for boosting pooled rideshare service. IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2025. Link
  • An integrated transportation-power system model for a decarbonizing world. Transportation Planning and Technology, 2024. Link

New directions in the ATLAS Lab

The ATLAS Lab, co-founded with Dr. Sudipta Chowdhury in the Department of Engineering Technology & Industrial Distribution, is named for where the work is heading: bringing AI and optimization together, and making logistics networks resilient.

Current focus

  • AI-assisted optimization: models that solve real business problems and explain their results and what-if scenarios to decision makers.
  • Resilient logistics networks with replenishment and range constraints.
  • Scalable algorithms (decomposition, column generation, parallel computing) for problems with millions of trips or trade pairs.
